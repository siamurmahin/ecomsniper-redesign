# Terms and Conditions — `/terms-and-conditions`

Checked 3 Sep 2026. **There is no copy to capture.**

The live page renders a heading and one line:

> EcomSniper – Terms and Conditions
> Last Updated: Invalid Date

Nothing between the header and the footer. Confirmed on a full load with a
screenshot — 423 characters in `<main>`, every one of them navigation or
footer chrome. Two paragraphs and eight list items in the DOM, all of it site
furniture.

"Invalid Date" is a literal — a date value that failed to parse, rendered to
visitors on a legal page.

**This page cannot be rebuilt from their site.** The client has to supply the
terms. Tracked as issue 2 in `../ISSUES.md`.

Worth noting the contrast: their affiliate programme has full terms at
`/affiliate/join`, which suggests the general terms were meant to exist and
were lost or never written, rather than deliberately omitted.
