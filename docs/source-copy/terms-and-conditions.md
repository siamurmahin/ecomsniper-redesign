## This file used to say the page was empty. It was wrong.

The 3 Sep capture recorded a heading, the line "Last Updated: Invalid Date",
and nothing else — 423 characters, all of it navigation and footer. That was
a page read before it had hydrated, and it was believed. It is the same
mistake as the About capture, and the fifth in this family; the count is in
`../SESSION-NOTES-04-SEP.md`.

The cost was not just a wrong note. `ISSUES.md` carried "there is no terms of
service, on a site taking $199 a month" as a high-severity finding about the
client, and the footer pointed at their live site because of it. Both are
corrected.

Before a line of the page was built, every clause here was checked against the
live DOM: **37 numbered clauses, 44 of 45 strings byte-identical** to what is
now in `src/content/en/legal.js`. The one difference is section 15, where
their page breaks the address over two lines and ours sets it as one.

## Three defects, reproduced rather than corrected

A contract is not ours to edit. All three are raised with the client in
`../ISSUES.md` instead.

1. **Clause 6.1 promises a flat 30-day refund from the date of purchase.** The
   client told us the refund is monthly-plan only, and every other page on the
   rebuild says so. Their binding document does not, and the binding document
   is the one that would be read out in a dispute.
2. **Clause 9.3 contains the literal string `[USD$100]`** — square brackets and
   all. An unfilled template placeholder inside a liability cap.
3. **Refunds are directed to `sammy@ecomsniper.io`**, a personal address, while
   the site’s published contact address is `management@ecomsniper.io`.
