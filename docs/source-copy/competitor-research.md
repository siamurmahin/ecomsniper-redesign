## Worth raising with the client

The whole page is built on undercutting named competitors — "snipe their
listing", "post yours at a slightly lower cost". That is their product and
their positioning, not something to soften unasked. But it is the one page
whose copy makes a claim _about how the tool treats other sellers_, and the
rebuild has been careful elsewhere about claims. Flagging it rather than
changing it.
